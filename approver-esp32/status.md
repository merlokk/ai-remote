# approver-esp32 — status, row by row

What is running on the board, what is written and untried, and what is still only
a design — one row per piece of the firmware, with the section that owns it. This
file carries **no decisions and no protocol**: it is the answer to "is this part
real yet", and every claim in it is meant to be checked against a section in
[`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)'s table of documents rather than trusted on its own.

The short form — the loop, the hardware under it, the screens over it, and the two
things that are not done — is the status summary in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), and
that is the one to read first. This is the long form, and it is the file that goes
stale fastest, so a row that disagrees with its own section should be believed
second.
